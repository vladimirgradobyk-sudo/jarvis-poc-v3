About This Report

This report aggregates sprint velocity logs and JARVIS run telemetry from the Intent domain — Bifrost and Rocket squads — across five releases (R64–R68, Jan–Jul 2026). Platform costs were computed from production run logs across all six skills. Team operating metrics (retrospectives, delivery milestones) were sourced from sprint review decks through R70 (Nov 2026). The Wave 2 Role Champions programme (Q1 2027) maps all ten PDLC functional areas to their AI focus, identifying which are actively served by JARVIS today and which are targeted for adoption in the next wave.

Gap Analysis — What Wave 2 Unlocks

Current JARVIS coverage addresses the core delivery pipeline: ideation (PRD) → requirements (User Stories) → design (HLD/LLD) → build & review (Implement/PR Review). Wave 2 extends AI PDLC to the quality, operations, and program management functions that sit upstream and downstream of that pipeline. Notably, Quality Engineering (automated test generation), SRE (observability & RCA), and Release Management (deployment gates) are the three highest-impact uncovered areas based on current delivery bottlenecks observed in R68–R70 retrospectives.

Maturity Arc

R66 (Learning): Teams invest in AI knowledge-sharing sessions, exploring capabilities.
R67 (Quality Risks): AI-generated content introduces prompt leakage, workarounds, and over-verbose stories. Teams recognize the quality trade-off.
R68 (Governance + Innovation): Guidelines established for AI-generated code review. Simultaneously, AI tooling (MCP, DevTools) matures enough for cross-team adoption and external showcase.
R69–R70 (Measurement): Teams recognize that AI reduces coding time but adds review overhead. The response is to measure the full lifecycle, not just generation speed — a sign of mature adoption.
